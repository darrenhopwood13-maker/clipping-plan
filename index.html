What kills it

Nearly every person who "tried clipping and it didn't work" hit one of these five.

  • Minimum view floors. A 1,000-view floor means most posts earn nothing. Pick campaigns with no floor for your first week.
  • The pool draining mid-flight. Money is committed when a clip is approved, not when you post it. Check what's left before you spend an evening editing.
  • Rejection after the views have banked. Missing hashtag, missing handle tag, your own logo on it, wrong length, clipped from outside their official folder. That is unpaid work you could have avoided in two minutes of reading.
  • Clips that need context. If it doesn't land in two seconds it dies, no matter how good the moment was.
  • The payout identity. The money lands in a verified payout account. In the UK that means an adult's PayPal or bank, in that adult's name.
